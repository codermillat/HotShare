# Acceptable Use

HotShare is general-purpose networking software: it shares an internet connection between devices
you own, over a network you are authorized to use. This page describes the use we intend and
support, and the use we ask you not to make of it.

## Intended use

- Sharing a connection **you are authorized to share** with **your own devices** — phone, laptop,
  tablet, or a local VM.
- Working where a second device has no direct connectivity: no mobile data, a guest network, or a
  lab/test environment.
- Local-network and development use: testing client/server setups across two devices.

## Please don't

- **Share or resell** a connection to other people as a service, or use it to operate a network
  offering for third parties. Doing so may require a licence in your country, and it is outside what
  this project is for.
- Use it on a network, or on a plan, that does not permit this kind of sharing.
- Use it to reach a service you have not paid for or are not authorized to use.

## Your responsibility

Your service agreement is between you and your provider. Some plans allow multi-device sharing,
some meter it, some prohibit it; where it is prohibited, using this software in that way is a
matter between you and your provider. The authors are not a party to your service agreement and
accept no liability for misuse. Where an official multi-device add-on exists, prefer it.

## Contributions

Changes whose purpose is to defeat a provider's or network operator's accounting, authentication,
or entitlement controls are not accepted into this project.
